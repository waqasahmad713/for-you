Drop your photos in this folder to activate the "See Our Memories" slideshow.

Naming:
  memory-1.jpg
  memory-2.jpg
  memory-3.jpg
  ... up to memory-10.jpg

.jpg, .jpeg, .png, and .webp all work. You don't need all 10 — even
2 or 3 photos is enough for the slideshow to appear. If this folder
is empty, the site still works perfectly; the memories button and
strip just stay hidden until you add photos.
